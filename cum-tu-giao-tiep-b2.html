100 Cụm Từ Giao Tiếp Tiếng Anh Thông Dụng Mức B2

Bộ 100 cụm từ cốt lõi không trùng lặp nghĩa để chuyển từ vốn từ thụ động sang phản xạ nói chủ động (đã bổ sung 10 cụm từ 91–100).

Hiển thị 100/100 cụm từ
STT Cụm từ Dịch nghĩa Ví dụ
1 From my perspective, ... Theo góc nhìn của tôi thì... From my perspective, consistency matters more than studying for five hours once a week.
2 To be honest, ... Thành thật mà nói... To be honest, I prefer staying home on weekends rather than going to crowded malls.
3 I couldn't agree more. Tôi hoàn toàn đồng ý. You said good sleep is the foundation of productivity? I couldn't agree more.
4 I see your point, but... Tôi hiểu ý bạn, nhưng... I see your point, but flying saves a whole day compared to taking the train.
5 Not necessarily. Không hẳn vậy đâu. Is the most expensive restaurant always the best? Not necessarily.
6 That makes a lot of sense. Nghe rất hợp lý. Practicing fifteen minutes every day instead of cramming once a week—that makes a lot of sense.
7 I'm in two minds about... Tôi đang phân vân giữa hai lựa chọn về... I'm in two minds about whether to buy a new laptop now or wait until next year.
8 I'm leaning towards... Tôi đang nghiêng về phương án... We haven't booked the trip yet, but I'm leaning towards going to the beach.
9 I don't mind + V-ing / N Tôi không phiền / Sao cũng được I don't mind driving if you're feeling tired today.
10 It's up to you / someone. Tùy bạn / Tùy ai đó quyết định. We can eat noodles or seafood tonight—it's up to you.
11 Take something for granted Coi điều gì là hiển nhiên (không biết trân trọng) Many people take their health for granted until they get sick.
12 It goes without saying that... Hiển nhiên là / Khỏi cần nói cũng biết rằng... It goes without saying that regular exercise improves your mood.
13 To be fair, ... Công bằng mà nói thì... The service was a bit slow, though to be fair, the restaurant was completely packed.
14 Generally speaking, ... Nói chung là... Generally speaking, public transport is cheaper, though less flexible than a motorbike.
15 Speaking of + N / which, ... Nhắc tới chuyện đó mới nhớ... You went to Nha Trang? Speaking of which, did you try the local seafood?
16 Off the top of my head, ... Theo trí nhớ nhanh của tôi thì... Off the top of my head, I'd say the meeting starts at 9:30.
17 How should I put this... Nên diễn đạt ý này thế nào nhỉ... How should I put this—he's very talented, just not great at teamwork.
18 In other words, ... Nói cách khác thì... The schedule is flexible. In other words, we can start whenever everyone is ready.
19 For instance, ... Chẳng hạn như... Small habits can save a lot of money; for instance, brewing coffee at home.
20 On top of that, ... Hơn thế nữa / Đã thế lại còn... The hotel was quiet and clean, and on top of that, the staff were super friendly.
21 Having said that, ... Tuy nói vậy nhưng... City life can be stressful. Having said that, I love having everything nearby.
22 On the one hand... on the other hand... Một mặt thì... mặt khác thì... On the one hand, working remotely saves commute time; on the other hand, it can feel isolating.
23 As a result, ... Kết quả là / Hệ quả là... It rained heavily all afternoon; as a result, the traffic was completely backed up.
24 At the end of the day, ... Suy cho cùng thì... We compared dozens of options, but at the end of the day, comfort was our top priority.
25 By the way, ... À nhân tiện... By the way, did you remember to send that email before noon?
26 Now that I think about it, ... Giờ nghĩ lại thì... Now that I think about it, I haven't seen my old classmate in almost ten years.
27 What stands out to me is... Điều làm tôi ấn tượng nhất là... What stands out to me is how calm she stays under pressure.
28 That reminds me of... Điều đó làm tôi nhớ tới... That smell of grilled corn reminds me of evening walks in my hometown.
29 It's a trade-off between A and B. Đó là sự đánh đổi giữa A và B. Choosing a home is always a trade-off between location and space.
30 The downside is (that)... Nhược điểm / Mặt trái là... The electric bike is super quiet, though the downside is its limited range.
31 The upside is (that)... Ưu điểm / Mặt tích cực là... The job is demanding, but the upside is that you learn very quickly.
32 It depends on... Điều đó còn tùy thuộc vào... Whether we jog outside or not depends on the weather.
33 It's worth + V-ing / N Rất đáng để làm gì / Đáng giá... Waking up early is tough, but watching the sunrise by the sea is worth it.
34 Live up to expectations Đạt được như kỳ vọng Everyone praised that movie, and honestly, it really lived up to my expectations.
35 As long as... Miễn là... I don't care where we eat as long as the place is clean.
36 Even though... Mặc dù... Even though I understand the grammar rules, I still hesitate when speaking.
37 Due to... Do / Bởi vì (+ danh từ) The flight was delayed for two hours due to heavy fog.
38 In case... Phòng khi / Đề phòng trường hợp... Bring a light jacket in case it gets chilly in the evening.
39 Make a big difference Tạo ra sự khác biệt lớn Drinking enough water every day can make a big difference to your energy levels.
40 Play a major role in... Đóng vai trò quan trọng trong... Good listening habits play a major role in improving your pronunciation.
41 In the long run Về lâu dài Jogging at a comfortable pace is much better for your knees in the long run.
42 In the short term Trong ngắn hạn Cutting costs helps in the short term, but we still need a growth plan.
43 Get into the habit of + V-ing Hình thành thói quen làm gì I've gotten into the habit of reading for twenty minutes before bed.
44 Used to + V Đã từng (thói quen cũ giờ không còn) I used to drink three cups of coffee a day, but now I only have one.
45 Get used to + V-ing / N Làm quen dần với... It took me a few weeks to get used to waking up at 5:30 AM.
46 Every now and then Thỉnh thoảng / Đôi khi I eat simply during the week, though I enjoy a big seafood meal every now and then.
47 Back in the day Ngày xưa / Hồi trước Back in the day, we had to look up every new word in a paper dictionary.
48 In the meantime Trong lúc chờ đợi / Trong thời gian đó Dinner will be ready in twenty minutes; in the meantime, help yourself to some fruit.
49 Sooner or later Sớm hay muộn thì cũng... If you practice speaking every day, your fluency will improve sooner or later.
50 At the last minute Vào phút chót He always tries to avoid packing at the last minute before a trip.
51 Run out of + N Hết sạch / Cạn kiệt cái gì We ran out of milk this morning, so I had my coffee black.
52 Take time to + V Cần thời gian / Dành thời gian để làm gì Building a new skill takes time, so don't be too hard on yourself.
53 All of a sudden Bỗng dưng / Đột nhiên The sky was completely clear, and then all of a sudden it started pouring.
54 Over time Theo thời gian / Dần dần Phrases that feel awkward at first will become completely natural over time.
55 Stick to + N / V-ing Kiên trì bám sát (kế hoạch/thói quen) It's easy to start a workout plan, but the real challenge is sticking to it.
56 Figure out Tìm ra cách giải quyết / Hiểu ra vấn đề It took me a while to figure out how to organize my study routine.
57 Deal with Xử lý / Đối phó với Part of living in a big city is learning how to deal with heavy traffic.
58 End up + V-ing / N / prep Cuối cùng lại rơi vào kết cục... We planned to walk for ten minutes, but we ended up exploring the whole park.
59 Turn out (to be / that) Hóa ra là / Kết quả lại thành ra... I was worried about the presentation, but it turned out much better than expected.
60 Come up with Nghĩ ra (ý tưởng / giải pháp) We need to come up with a simpler way to remember new expressions.
61 Keep track of Theo dõi sát sao / Nắm bắt thông tin I use a small notebook to keep track of my monthly expenses.
62 Make up my mind Đưa ra quyết định dứt khoát After thinking about it for a week, I finally made up my mind to take the course.
63 Catch up with Gặp gỡ hỏi thăm / Bắt kịp tiến độ I met an old friend for coffee yesterday to catch up with him.
64 Look forward to + V-ing / N Mong chờ điều gì I'm really looking forward to taking a few days off next month.
65 Put off Trì hoãn Don't put off practicing until you feel 'ready'—just start today.
66 Cut down on Cắt giảm (tiêu thụ / thói quen không tốt) I'm trying to cut down on sugar and late-night snacks.
67 Give up on Từ bỏ (hy vọng / nỗ lực với ai hoặc việc gì) Even when progress felt slow, she never gave up on her goal.
68 Make the most of Tận dụng tối đa Since the weather is cool today, let's make the most of it and go outside.
69 Keep in mind (that)... Lưu ý / Ghi nhớ rằng... Keep in mind that native speakers use simple phrases most of the time.
70 Point out Chỉ ra (một lỗi sai hoặc chi tiết quan trọng) My tutor pointed out a small pronunciation mistake I had been making for years.
71 Go through Trải qua (giai đoạn) / Xem kỹ lại Almost every language learner goes through a plateau around level B1.
72 Pay off Đem lại kết quả xứng đáng / Đền đáp công sức All those months of steady practice are finally starting to pay off.
73 Burn out Kiệt sức (vì làm việc / học tập quá tải) If you try to memorize fifty words a day, you'll burn out within a week.
74 Fall behind Bị tụt lại phía sau (so với tiến độ) I missed two classes and felt like I was starting to fall behind.
75 Get along with Hòa thuận / Hợp tính với ai She's easygoing and gets along with almost everyone in the office.
76 Keep in touch (with) Giữ liên lạc với ai Even though my relatives live abroad, we still keep in touch through messaging apps.
77 Feel like + V-ing / N Có hứng / Cảm thấy muốn làm gì I don't feel like cooking tonight—let's grab something simple nearby.
78 Can't afford + to V / N Không đủ khả năng chi trả (tiền / thời gian) With a busy schedule, I can't afford to waste two hours stuck in traffic.
79 Be supposed to + V Đáng lẽ phải / Được kỳ vọng là sẽ... The train was supposed to arrive at 7:00, but it came twenty minutes late.
80 Tend to + V Có xu hướng làm gì When I speak too fast, I tend to drop the final sounds of words.
81 Manage to + V Xoay xở làm được (việc khó) Despite the noisy café, we still managed to have a great conversation.
82 Be in charge of Phụ trách / Chịu trách nhiệm quản lý She's in charge of coordinating the entire customer support team.
83 Look back (on) Nhìn lại (quá khứ / trải nghiệm) Looking back on my first marathon, I'm amazed I actually finished it.
84 Can't stand + V-ing / N Không thể chịu nổi... I really can't stand waiting in long lines under the hot sun.
85 Be into + N / V-ing Thích thú / Đam mê cái gì I'm not really into extreme sports; I prefer steady daily running.
86 Get rid of Loại bỏ / Bỏ đi thứ không cần thiết I spent Sunday morning getting rid of old clothes and clutter.
87 Run into Tình cờ gặp ai / Gặp phải vấn đề I ran into my former neighbor at the supermarket this morning.
88 Have a hard time + V-ing Gặp khó khăn khi làm gì Many learners have a hard time turning passive vocabulary into active speech.
89 Can't help + V-ing Không thể nhịn được / Không ngăn mình khỏi... Whenever I hear that song, I can't help smiling.
90 Might as well + V Thôi thì cứ... luôn cho rồi / Đằng nào cũng... Since we're already downtown, we might as well stop by the bookstore.
91 When it comes to + N / V-ing Khi nói đến (lĩnh vực / việc gì)... When it comes to learning a language, daily practice beats talent every time.
92 In terms of + N Về mặt / Xét về khía cạnh... The apartment is quite small, but in terms of location, it's unbeatable.
93 As far as I know, ... Theo như tôi biết thì... As far as I know, the park stays open until 10 PM every night.
94 No wonder (+ mệnh đề) Thảo nào / Hèn gì... You slept only four hours last night? No wonder you look so tired today.
95 Either way, ... Dù theo cách nào đi nữa... We can take the highway or the scenic route—either way, it'll be a nice trip.
96 There's no point (in) + V-ing Chẳng ích gì khi làm... There's no point in worrying about small grammar mistakes while you're speaking.
97 Rely on Dựa vào / Trông cậy vào... I rely on my calendar app to remember all my appointments and errands.
98 Bring up Đề cập đến / Nêu ra (một chủ đề) I'm glad you brought that up—I've been meaning to ask you about it.
99 Look into Tìm hiểu kỹ / Xem xét (một vấn đề / lựa chọn) That sounds like a solid option; I'll look into the details this weekend.
100 Get over Vượt qua (nỗi buồn, cú sốc) / Khỏi bệnh It took me almost two weeks to completely get over that bad flu.
