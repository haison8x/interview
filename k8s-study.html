Kubernetes Study Guide – 60 Questions Explained

Long-form study notes for every question on the Kubernetes interview page, split into five parts. Each question starts with the short answer, then explains the mechanism step by step, walks through a concrete example, lists the commands and what they really do, and ends with common pitfalls and how to phrase the answer in an interview. Targets Kubernetes 1.35.

1. Basics

Questions 1–11

  1. What are the components of a Kubernetes cluster?
  2. What happens, end to end, when you run kubectl apply on a Deployment?
  3. What is a Pod, and why does Kubernetes schedule pods, not containers?
  4. What is kubectl, and which commands do you use daily?
  5. What is a Deployment?
  6. What is a Service, and why is it needed?
  7. What Service types are there?
  8. ConfigMap vs Secret – and are Secrets actually secret?
  9. What are Namespaces?
  10. What are labels and selectors?
  11. Explain PV, PVC and StorageClass. Why is my pod Pending on a volume?

2. Networking, Config, Resources, Rollouts

Questions 12–26

  1. How does Kubernetes networking work?
  2. How does a ClusterIP actually route traffic? Nothing listens on that IP.
  3. How does RBAC work, and how do pods authenticate to the API and the cloud?
  4. HPA, VPA, Cluster Autoscaler – how do they fit together? Traffic doubled but HPA didn't scale – why?
  5. How do you debug a failing pod?
  6. Liveness vs readiness vs startup probes – and how can a probe cause an outage?
  7. How does a rolling update work, and how do you roll back?
  8. What is an Ingress, and how does it work?
  9. What is the Gateway API, how is it different from Ingress – and what happened to ingress-nginx?
  10. Requests vs limits – and what are QoS classes?
  11. What happens when a container exceeds its limits?
  12. Should you set CPU limits?
  13. What are init containers, and when should you use them?
  14. What is a PodDisruptionBudget, and how do you keep apps highly available?
  15. What is a NetworkPolicy, and why might yours do nothing?

3. Advanced Workloads and Extensibility

Questions 27–38

  1. What is a StatefulSet, and how does it differ from a Deployment?
  2. What is a DaemonSet, and when would you use it?
  3. Jobs and CronJobs – what should you know?
  4. What is a service mesh, and why use one?
  5. How do you control where pods run? Taints, tolerations, affinity, topology spread.
  6. What are sidecar containers, and how are they used?
  7. What are native sidecars, and what problem do they solve?
  8. What are admission webhooks and admission policies?
  9. How do you achieve zero-downtime deployments? Why do users still get errors during a rolling update?
  10. What are Custom Resource Definitions (CRDs)?
  11. What is an operator, and how does it work?
  12. What is in-place pod resize?

4. Cluster Administration

Questions 39–49

  1. How do you back up and restore etcd?
  2. How do you upgrade a cluster safely?
  3. How do you monitor a cluster?
  4. How do you set up logging?
  5. How do you prevent pods from running privileged or as root?
  6. How do you secure a Kubernetes cluster overall?
  7. How do you make the cluster itself highly available?
  8. How do you encrypt Secrets at rest in etcd?
  9. Several teams share one cluster. How do you stop one team affecting another?
  10. ResourceQuota vs LimitRange?
  11. How does DNS (CoreDNS) work, and why might lookups be slow?

5. Production Scenarios

Questions 50–60

  1. An application in Kubernetes has become slow. How do you investigate?
  2. Nginx pods are Running, but the application is unreachable via its URL. What do you check?
  3. A new version was deployed and fails to start, or the rollout is stuck. How do you investigate and recover?
  4. A microservice can't connect to an external database outside the cluster. How do you fix it?
  5. New pods stay Pending with "0/3 nodes are available: Insufficient cpu, Insufficient memory". Walk me through it.
  6. A pod is in CrashLoopBackOff. What do you do?
  7. Pods are Running but not Ready, or the Service returns intermittent 503s.
  8. Pods restart during traffic spikes with OOMKilled. Or they get Evicted. What's the difference?
  9. ImagePullBackOff / ErrImagePull – what's wrong?
  10. A node becomes NotReady. What happens to its pods?
  11. How do you deploy to Kubernetes in production? What is GitOps?
